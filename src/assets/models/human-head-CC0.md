# Anatomical head asset

Derived from the body group of MakeHuman base.obj, explicitly released as CC0 in September 2020. The head and upper neck faces are retained in human-head.js. Body faces are split into limb regions, triangulated, scaled to metres, and quantized into human-body.bin. Garment regions receive additional ease, and the torso garment is replaced by an original loose coat shell. No MakeHuman program code is included.
